1. Scope
This policy describes the privacy practices of the GroovyCodex for SAP CPI plugin (the “Plugin”), which helps users create, explain, review, and troubleshoot Groovy scripts for SAP Integration Suite Cloud Integration.
The Plugin is currently a skills-only assistant. It does not connect to an SAP tenant, execute scripts, or operate a separate server, database, or external integration.
2. Information handled when you use the Plugin
You may choose to include prompts, Groovy code, sample payloads, headers or properties, and error messages in your ChatGPT conversation. The Plugin does not independently collect or receive that conversation content, and its publisher does not operate a system that stores it.
ChatGPT and OpenAI process conversation content to provide the service. Their handling, retention, and privacy controls are governed by the terms and privacy policy applicable to your ChatGPT account. See OpenAI's Privacy Policy.
3. How the publisher uses or shares information
The publisher does not receive or use Plugin conversations for analytics, advertising, profiling, or independent model training. The Plugin does not send conversation content to SAP or another third party.
4. Retention and deletion
Because the publisher does not receive or store Plugin conversations, the publisher has no separate copy to retain or delete. Manage or delete your conversation through the controls available in your ChatGPT account. OpenAI's applicable policies describe its retention and deletion practices.
5. Information you should not include
Do not submit passwords, access tokens, private keys, production credentials, or unredacted personal, customer, or confidential business data. Use sanitized examples and follow your employer's data-handling requirements. The Plugin cannot access or test your SAP tenant.
6. Children
The Plugin is intended for professional and developer use, not for children under 13. Do not use it to collect personal information from children under 13.
7. Changes to this policy
If the Plugin's data handling changes, this policy will be updated before or when the change takes effect. The effective date above will be revised.
