Enhancing Global HR Automation with SAP SuccessFactors and Zero Trust Security

Rameshkumar Varanganti

International Journal of Marketing and Human Resource Management, Vol. 16, Issue 3, pp. 44–73, 2025 · IAEME Publication

Download PDF DOI: 10.34218/ijmhrm_16_03_004

Abstract

Examines how global enterprises can automate HR processes on SAP SuccessFactors while protecting sensitive employee data across regions, by applying Zero Trust security principles to integrations, identity and access, and data flows. It covers the integration architecture, security controls and testing approach for such deployments.

Keywords

SAP SuccessFactors; Zero Trust security; HR automation; SAP Cloud Integration; Identity and access management
